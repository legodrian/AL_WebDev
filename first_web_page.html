Timeline

Image from:MoMA

Minecraft is a sandbox game launched publicly as an early-access alpha on May 17, 2009, and had its official full release on November 18, 2011. Originately it was created by the Sewdish video game programmer and designer Markus Notch Persson. And later on, in September of 2014 Mojang Studios (The company that created minecraft) and minecraft were acquired by Microsoft for $2.5 billion. Some people who have important role on minecraft developed are Jens Jeb Bergensten, chief Creative Officer at Mojang Studios, who took over as lead designer and guides the game's updates. And Agnes LadyAgnes Larsson, Vanilla (Minecraft version for PC) Game Director for Minecraft at Mojang Studios. Although multiple games have tried to take minecraft's place, like the most famous one Hytale, the game is still intact.

Minecraft today

Image from:Dashboard Icons & Logos

Today, after 17 years (my age) of its creation, minecraft is considered one of the best-selling videogames ever created, selling about 425 millions of copies worldwide. Being able to play the game in multiple devices and platforms such as:

  • Mobile (iOS / Android) with around 88 million players.
  • PC (Windows / Mac / Linux) with around 62 million players.
  • Consoles (PlayStation, Xbox) with around 43 million players.
  • Nintendo Switch with around 19 million players.
With icons of the minecraft culture like: Dream, famous for his Manhunt series and the Dream SMP role-play server, and Technoblade, a beloved, legendary competitive PvP player and content creator who passed away in 2022. The latest official release is Minecraft Bedrock Edition 26.60, which launched on October 27, 2026. The update is named The Ice Update (Part 1).
