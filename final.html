What is Moab ?

Moab is a world-renowned desert adventure town in southeastern Utah that sits along the banks of the Colorado River, serving as the primary gateway to both Arches and Canyonlands National Parks.
Framed by towering red sandstone cliffs, deep canyons, and the snow-capped La Sal Mountains, the area evolved from an Indigenous homeland, Mormon farming settlement, and 1950s uranium mining hub into a global mecca for outdoor recreation. Today, this small, laid-back community attracts millions of visitors each year who come to hike, mountain bike, off-road, raft, and stargaze beneath its pristine desert skies.

Why you should go to Moab?

People visit Moab, Utah for its dramatic red rock landscapes and direct access to iconic parks like Arches National Park, Canyonlands National Park, and Dead Horse Point State Park. Beyond sightseeing, the area serves as a premier outdoor hub for hiking, mountain biking, rock climbing, off-roading, and whitewater rafting along the Colorado River. Visitors are also drawn to its dark skies for stargazing, ancient rock art and dinosaur tracks, and a welcoming downtown filled with local shops, outfitters, and dining spots to unwind after a day in the desert.

Fun fact: "MOAB" can also refer to the GBU-43/B Massive Ordnance Air Blast, a large-yield
conventional bomb developed by the U.S. military, or equipment rental software.

What can I do in Moab ?

Some things you can do in Moab include:

Parks & Scenic Viewpoints

  1. Arches National Park (including Delicate Arch, the Windows Section, and Devils Garden)
  2. Canyonlands National Park (Island in the Sky district & Mesa Arch)
  3. Dead Horse Point State Park (Colorado River overlook & trails)

Hikes & Waterfalls

  1. Corona Arch (trail outside the national park)
  2. Mill Creek Waterfall (canyon hike and swimming hole)
  3. Faux Falls (waterfall hike)

Outdoor Adventures & Trails

  1. Off-Roading & 4x4 Jeep/Hummer Tours (including Hell's Revenge and Top of the World)
  2. Whitewater Rafting & Kayaking (on the Colorado River)
  3. Mountain Biking (including the Slickrock Bike Trail and The Whole Enchilada)

Local Sights & Family Attractions

  1. Moab Giants (outdoor dinosaur trail and museum)
  2. Hole 'N' The Rock (carved sandstone home and petting zoo)
  3. Downtown Main Street (including the Moab Rock Shop, local dining, and shops)

Hotels in Moab, Utah range from budget-friendly motels and familiar chain properties along US-191 to upscale boutique stays and full-service downtown resorts. Nightly rates fluctuate heavily by season, dropping to roughly $50 to $170 during quieter winter periods and climbing between $200 and $510+ during the peak spring and fall travel months. Travelers can choose between practical, value-oriented chains like Aarchway Inn, Wingate by Wyndham, and SpringHill Suites—which typically offer free breakfast, pools, and oversized parking for trailers or RVs—or higher-end downtown options like Hoodoo Moab and Gonzo Inn for spa services, dining, and easy walkability.
