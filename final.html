What is Moab ?

Moab is a world-renowned desert adventure town in southeastern Utah that sits along the banks of the Colorado River, serving as the primary gateway to both Arches and Canyonlands National Parks.
Framed by towering red sandstone cliffs, deep canyons, and the snow-capped La Sal Mountains, the area evolved from an Indigenous homeland, Mormon farming settlement, and 1950s uranium mining hub into a global
mecca for outdoor recreation. Today, this small, laid-back community attracts millions of visitors each year who come to hike, mountain bike, off-road, raft, and stargaze beneath its pristine desert skies.

Image from:Wikimedia Commons

Fun fact: "MOAB" can also refer to the GBU-43/B Massive Ordnance Air Blast, a large-yield
conventional bomb developed by the U.S. military, or equipment rental software.

What can I do in Moab ?

Some things you can do in Moab include:

Parks & Scenic Viewpoints

  1. Arches National Park (including Delicate Arch, the Windows Section, and Devils Garden)
  2. Canyonlands National Park (Island in the Sky district & Mesa Arch)
  3. Dead Horse Point State Park (Colorado River overlook & trails)

Hikes & Waterfalls

  1. Corona Arch (trail outside the national park)
  2. Mill Creek Waterfall (canyon hike and swimming hole)
  3. Faux Falls (waterfall hike)

Outdoor Adventures & Trails

  1. Off-Roading & 4x4 Jeep/Hummer Tours (including Hell's Revenge and Top of the World)
  2. Whitewater Rafting & Kayaking (on the Colorado River)
  3. Mountain Biking (including the Slickrock Bike Trail and The Whole Enchilada)

Local Sights & Family Attractions

  1. Moab Giants (outdoor dinosaur trail and museum)
  2. Hole 'N' The Rock (carved sandstone home and petting zoo)
  3. Downtown Main Street (including the Moab Rock Shop, local dining, and shops)
